An independent literary publication

A place for work that asks for attention.

We publish long-form thought, reflective writing and creative work concerned with the inner movements of being human. The work is not designed to inform at speed or motivate on command. It is offered as an invitation to pause, notice and consider.

Our editorial interests include psychological clarity, consciousness, memory, faith, identity, attention, transformation and the relationship between inner life and outward action.

“Some work is not meant to be consumed quickly. It is meant to be lived with.”

The Living Current

The Living Current explores the quiet order that governs movement, timing and coherence in human life. It is not a system for self-improvement, productivity or belief. It offers no guarantees, shortcuts or techniques. Instead, it invites the reader to observe how alignment forms when internal truth and external action stop conflicting.

Lucien Sage

Lucien Sage is the author of The Living Current and the originating voice behind the work published through Loomcast. His work returns to questions of awareness, alignment, silence, faith, transformation and the relationship between what a person knows inwardly and how they move through the world. The writing is reflective rather than prescriptive. It does not ask for allegiance or offer certainty in place of inquiry.

Lucien maintains a deliberate degree of privacy. The work is intended to stand on its own, and the reader is free to meet it without needing to follow a personality.

The Circle of Keepers

There is an old idea that not all knowledge is taught. Some of it is remembered. In certain traditions, the keeper was not a leader or a teacher, but a witness, someone who stood at the edge of meaning, preserving what could not be rushed or translated without loss. This work comes from that orientation. Not to instruct, persuade, or convert, but to hold a certain frequency intact. If that means nothing to you, that’s fine. If it does, you already understand enough.
