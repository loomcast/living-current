The Living Current

By Lucien Sage · Published by Loomcast

The Living Current explores the quiet order that governs movement, timing and coherence in human life. Across twelve chapters, it considers alignment, balance, silence, faith, transformation and awareness. It does not offer a formula for living. It invites the reader to observe more closely and reconsider familiar perceptions.
